Freedom Arkives Hebrew

Official Reconstruction — Edition 1.0 · Adopted October 23, 2026

Freedom Arkives Hebrew is the official reconstructed Hebrew register for Freedom Arkives publications, educational materials and POM-assisted translation. This is the organization's adopted living language standard.

Governing authority

The Hebrew Combined Study Compendium determines our primary Romanized vocabulary, spellings, variations and linguistic register. POM serves as the working language engine: it combines those forms with systematically developed grammar and comparisons to older and contemporary Hebrew to produce usable vocabulary, speech and translations.

Freedom Arkives takes responsibility for constructing and maintaining the language. Readers and users are not required to be linguists or individually approve every generated word, grammatical inflection or sentence.

How the language works

Established Compendium words retain their source spellings. Where the Compendium has no entry, POM derives a consistent form using the organization's current grammar conventions and relevant Hebrew comparison data. Those constructed forms belong to the official Freedom Arkives reconstruction, rather than being held indefinitely as drafts.

Translation and conversation

POM should translate complete sentences, passages and conversations into this official register, explain meaning when asked, and use Hebrew conversationally on request. Its lexicon, morphology, historical comparison and grammar datasets operate behind the conversation. Users do not need to configure conjugations or select grammatical rules to receive a translation.

Historical evidence and organizational authority

Our designation official reconstruction identifies the standard adopted by Freedom Arkives. It does not mean every newly constructed form is independently attested in an ancient text, nor does it establish one historically certain pronunciation. The project keeps source evidence distinguishable from its own linguistic constructions for accuracy and future edition maintenance, without imposing a user approval gate.

Freedom Arkives may refine the standard through documented, versioned editions. Such maintenance is the publisher's responsibility and does not suspend the language's present official status.
