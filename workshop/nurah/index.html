NURAH Protocols

The reasoning disciplines behind every Freedom Arkives publication.

Specified protocol, approved architecture. These five disciplines structure reasoning, historical comparison, dialectics, and questioning across the framework. Their performance in live publishing remains subject to ongoing testing — the disciplines are the standard; the results are the experiment.
PROTOCOL 01

Ethical Counter-Conditioning

Before any research begins, five checks must pass: no deception as a method, no military-coercion mechanisms adapted to civilian publishing, counterevidence actively sought, no claim exceeding what its source establishes, and no private motive asserted without evidence. Ethics here is not a statement of values — it is a blocking gate in the workflow. Deception and coercion are studied for understanding, never deployed as technique.

Lives in: Discovery → Verification gate · enforced as data, not decoration.
PROTOCOL 02

Trapdoor Narrative Structure

Every publication follows the same arc: hook → context → tension → explanation → pivot → Socratic close. Open with the debate people are already fighting about — the mass-appeal entry point nobody has to be convinced to care about. Then pivot into the framework. The audience arrives for the fight and stays for the substance. History documents the pattern: value first, pivot second.

Lives in: Narrative construction · the engine's narrative screen follows this field order.
PROTOCOL 03

Competing Arguments

Every claim is built as a dialectic: thesis, strongest antithesis, warranted synthesis. The antithesis must pass a steelman check — it has to be the strongest version of the opposing case, not a strawman. And no conclusion is forced: unresolved and insufficient evidence are valid, honorable terminal states. A forced synthesis is just marketing with footnotes.

Lives in: Dialectics stage · counterevidence surfaced inline, never buried.
PROTOCOL 04

Socratic Questioning

Every publication closes with a question, not a verdict. The question does the work of ten paragraphs: it transfers the thinking to the reader instead of delivering a conclusion for them to applaud or reject. A reader who answers the question has done the reasoning themselves — and reasoning they did themselves, they keep.

Lives in: Narrative close · a required, QC-validated field. No Socratic close, no seal.
PROTOCOL 05

Evidence-Based Conclusions

Every claim carries its evidence grade: PRIMARY, SCHOLARSHIP, INTERPRETATION, or PROVISIONAL. A cited source proves only what that source actually establishes — a large bibliography is not a substitute for that check. Uncertainty travels with the claim, in public, as part of the publication — not hidden in footnotes nobody reads.

Lives in: Verification ledger → every downstream stage · provisional flags are never stripped.
