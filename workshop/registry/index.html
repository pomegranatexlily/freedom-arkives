Strategic Mechanism Registry

22 historical mechanisms · WORKAVISM × NURAH PROTOCOLS × G-CAFL-QC FRAMEWORK™

Approved architectural baseline. These mechanisms are incorporated into the framework as the official baseline. Approval is not empirical verification — each mechanism's performance remains subject to testing under the measurement discipline: behavioral metrics only, reach is never presented as effect.

WM-001MODERATE

Monumental Repetition

Egypt

Repetition across durable surfaces outlasts any single proclamation.

Testable: Test: does repeated-formula content outperform one-off posts on recall? Measure: unaided recall survey.

WM-002STRONG

Dynastic Continuity Curation

Egypt

Curated continuity signals stability; erasure (damnatio) signals rupture.

Testable: Test: does an explicit series arc raise returning-reader rate vs standalone posts? Measure: returning readers.

WM-003STRONG

Visual Documentation of Power

Assyria

Images travel where text cannot; artifacts survive their makers.

Testable: Test: do image-led posts earn more meaningful reads than text-only? Measure: completion rate.

Reputation as Deterrence

Assyria

ADAPTATION EXCLUDED: fear-through-violence core not portable. Only credibility-through-demonstrated-rigor may transfer.

Testable: Test (residue only): does citing primary sources raise trust scores? Measure: trust survey.

Persia

WM-006MODERATE

Infrastructure as Communication System

Persia

Build the publishing operation to persist beyond individual proclamations.

Testable: Test: does a fixed cadence outperform irregular bursts on subscriber growth? Measure: subscriptions.

WM-007MODERATE

Public Challenge / Morale Speech

Israel/Judah

Testable: Test: does a direct-address post change reply sentiment vs neutral framing? Measure: reply analysis.

WM-008WEAK

Testable: Test: does non-response to bait reduce pile-on duration? Measure: thread lifespan.

WM-022STRONG

Reach ≠ Effect Measurement Discipline

Meta (all systems)

Reach is measurable, persuasion mostly isn't. No reach metric may be presented as an effect metric.

Testable: Governs all tests: every experiment must name its behavioral metric up front.
