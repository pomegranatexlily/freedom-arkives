Privacy Policy

Pomegranate × Lily · Updated October 13, 2026

Freedom Arkives is a public website for articles, books, historical research and publishing tools. This policy describes the current website implementation, rather than promising no data is ever processed.

Site hosting

Our website is served through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical connection information including IP address, request date, and browser details as needed to deliver pages, protect the service and maintain logs. This happens even if you reject optional features.

Optional third-party resources

Some article preview images are delivered through Medium's image CDN, and optional web fonts are served through Google Fonts. These external downloads are disabled until you choose to allow optional external resources. If enabled, the providers receive ordinary connection information. Clicking a link to Medium, Amazon, Linktree or another external website takes you to that provider, where its own privacy policy applies.

Shared-hostname storage limitation

Freedom Arkives currently shares pomegranatexlily.github.io with Python Atelier and other projects. Browser local storage belongs to the entire hostname; URL paths alone do not isolate it. Scripts running on this hostname may technically access another project's browser storage. Avoid recording passwords or highly sensitive personal details in Workshop drafts. We recommend separate origins before introducing accounts or sensitive services.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We do not currently run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, third-party behavioral profiling, or optional analytics scripts. We store a small preference record in your browser's local storage to remember your choice. Rejecting optional resources does not block the public content. Any future optional tracking must require a fresh consent decision before being activated.

Workshop tools and local storage

Some interactive tools within the Workshop may save drafts or preferences in your browser to support their basic function. Those local records are not uploaded to a Freedom Arkives application server by the present website code. Clearing your browser's site storage can erase locally saved work. Export important work first.

Your choices

Select Reject optional, Accept all, or Manage choices when the notice appears. You can reopen your choice using the Privacy choices control displayed on the site. If storage is unavailable, the choice may last only during the current visit. You can also clear stored site data using browser settings.

Contact

For questions about this website or this notice, contact the maintainers through the Freedom Arkives GitHub repository. GitHub issues are public; do not include sensitive personal information.
